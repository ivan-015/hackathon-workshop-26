Tips to win

Problem first

Who hurts and how, before any tech.

Prioritize your main feature

One thing clearly working beats five half-built features.

Happy path working

Make sure your happy path and demo is working 100%.

No stalls

Mock slow calls, pre-fill forms, seed demo data.

Public URL

A localhost-only demo can score as if it doesn’t work.

Backup video

Record it early so there’s time to redo it.

The test: after one minute, could a judge explain your project, who it’s for, and why it needs AI?

This comes from what hackathon judges keep saying: projects win when they’re legible. Lead with the problem, show one thing clearly working fast, be honest about limits. Everything in this pipeline, the public URL, the green checks, the golden-path test, exists so this 90 seconds goes smoothly.
